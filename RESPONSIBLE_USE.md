# Responsible Use Policy

Giggle Studio includes advanced reverse engineering and code analysis capabilities designed strictly for defensive cybersecurity, legitimate software engineering, and educational research.

---

## 1. Authorized Use Cases

The Reverse Engineering Suite and AI-assisted analysis tools are strictly intended for:
- **Authorized Vulnerability Research:** Auditing applications, firmware, and code that you own or have explicit written permission to assess.
- **Malware Triage & Defensive Analysis:** Investigating malicious binaries, suspicious email attachments, or software artifacts in an isolated quarantine environment to extract IOCs, YARA rules, and defensive mitigation signatures.
- **Interoperability & Legacy Maintenance:** Auditing legacy software, protocols, or device drivers where original documentation is missing or unavailable.
- **Capture The Flag (CTF) & Education:** Participating in cybersecurity training, academic coursework, and defense simulations.

---

## 2. Prohibited Non-Goals & Misuse

Giggle Studio explicitly forbids and rejects the following non-goals:
- Automatic weaponization or exploit generation.
- Authoring, enhancing, or distributing malware, ransomware, rootkits, or botnets.
- Developing attack payloads or offensive exploitation scripts.
- Bypassing software licensing, digital rights management (DRM), or copy-protection mechanisms.
- Analyzing software, networks, or infrastructure without proper legal authorization.

---

## 3. First-Run Acknowledgment

Before using the Reverse Engineering modules in Giggle Studio, users must explicitly acknowledge and agree:
1. I will only analyze binaries and software that I own, maintain, or have explicit authorization to inspect.
2. I will not use Giggle Studio to produce functional attack payloads, exploits, or malware.
3. I understand that dynamic analysis carries inherent risks and must only be conducted inside isolated virtual environments.
